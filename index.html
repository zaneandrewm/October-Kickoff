Where is Wednesday's kickoff?

At the Omni Austin Hotel Downtown, 700 San Jacinto Blvd (at E. 8th Street), in the Pennybacker conference room. Breakfast tacos and coffee or tea start at 8:30 AM. Please be seated by 8:55 AM so the program can start promptly at 9:00 AM, and we'll wrap by 12:30 PM.

Where should I park?

The hotel is only about a 10-minute walk from the Austin office. Hotel parking is $55, so it may be best to park at the office and walk over.

When do I need to arrive?

Plan to arrive on Tuesday, October 20. The sales org happy hour starts at 5:00 PM, though that time is still tentative.

Are team dinners required?

No. Team dinners on Tuesday and Thursday are optional. Teams that don't have dinner on Tuesday can have one on Thursday. Wednesday night's dinner and fun at Dave & Buster's is for everyone.

When can I fly home?

Thursday night and Friday are both fly-out times. Choose whichever works for you.

Where is the agenda?

Wednesday's agenda is posted in the schedule above. Thursday's workshop details are coming soon, so check back.

Where do I stay?

Many teams are staying at our usual hotels. Please check with your leader for your team's plan.

Will there be time to sell?

Yes. Thursday workshops only take up part of the day so there is focused selling time built in.
